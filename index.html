How TaskCampus works

Simple, fast, and safe — just for your campus.

01
Post a task
Describe what you need, set the amount you'll pay, and add a deadline. It goes live instantly.
02
Someone accepts
The first campus student to accept gets the job. Your task is locked to them — nobody else can grab it.
03
Task gets done
The doer completes the task and marks it done. An OTP is sent to you to confirm delivery.
04
Pay safely
After OTP verification, payment is released. The doer gets 80%, platform keeps 20%.
